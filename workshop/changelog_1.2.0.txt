v1.2.0 - Fast, aggressive attack runs

FASTER, DEADLIER
- Drones fly the attack themselves instead of leaving it to the AI pilot: 180 km/h for Anti-Tank, 144 km/h for Anti-Personnel
- Kills in 2-10 s after spotting a target, depending on distance (was 35-65 s)
- Moving vehicles get caught: drones lead their target and hit cars crossing at 70 km/h
- Charges go off on contact with the hull instead of a few metres short - roughly twice the damage to tanks
- Drones dive steeply over cover: a tank parked behind a building still gets hit
- A target driving away is chased well past the detection range
- Swarms spread out over the targets instead of all diving on the nearest one, and keep clear of each other
- A drone heading for a vehicle whose crew was just killed picks a live target instead
- Waypoints (e.g. set in Zeus) are flown at ~125 km/h instead of 30-60 km/h

NEW
- "Attack Speed" setting (km/h) on every module
- "Target Sides" setting: attack the mission's enemies (default), one side only, or everyone except the drone's own side

FIXED
- Zeus: a drone taken over while Zeus was still creating its crew could sit there forever in multiplayer

REMOVED
- Attack Distance, Horizontal Attack Distance, Final Attack Height and the delay/stuck settings - the attack run is flown by the script now, so they did nothing. Missions that set them still load fine.
