v1.1.0 - Zeus, multiplayer, and drones that actually hit

ZEUS (most requested)
- Modules now work in Zeus: drop one on a drone, or place it as a "drone station" - every drone put near it is picked up, including ones placed later
- New "Control All Drones Of This Type" (on by default): one module takes over every drone of its type on the map, like the original version did
- Zeus-placed drones work on dedicated servers (the server takes them over)
- Drones fly their waypoints while searching and attack what they find

FIXES
- Empty drones placed in the editor now get a crew automatically (they used to just sit there)
- Anti-Personnel module now actually targets infantry - its presets weren't being applied
- Drones dive into the target instead of hovering above it or crashing short - much higher kill rate
- Drones no longer break off their attack while taking off
- Multiplayer: explosions happen once on the server, not once per player
- No more chat spam by default (new "Enable Chat Messages" setting)
- Uses the mission's real side relations (independent works)
- A module can no longer take over a manned helicopter

NEW
- Signed with a .bikey for servers
- Two example scenarios (Play > Showcases), with editable copies in the mod's examples folder
